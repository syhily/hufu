at the cursor.

See colors, type size and corners as you edit.

Single characters first. Words when you want them.

Built on RIME. Open source.

Tiger-Code only, built on librime. Reads existing RIME configuration and imports vocabulary, phrases and colour schemes from Squirrel.

Original code MIT · dependencies keep their licences

GitHub · syhily/hufu

Everyday typing stays on your Mac.

Composition, candidates and personal word frequency stay on your Mac. No telemetry. Snippets stay local. Typing statistics are off by default.

AI defaults to Apple’s on-device model, which depends on your device, system settings and region (currently unavailable for mainland China devices and accounts). Otherwise, configure your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint (may be billed).

Privacy details

Call AI when you need it.

Press ⌃⌥P, or hold ⌥ and choose AI from the quick menu, to translate or polish the text you choose. Add your own actions too.

Simplified/Traditional conversion runs locally and needs no AI.

Use keys for frequent text.

Uses native librime. Organize snippets and assign phrase codes. Pick symbols with letter keys. Frequent words appear first.
