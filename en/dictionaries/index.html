Where the tables come from.

The vocabulary comes from the official Tiger-Code release 2026.08.15 (dicts/huma) and is built with the installer. The three word tables are prebuilt, so switching modes never recompiles them.

The Tiger-Code tables are used under their official release terms; brand entries grant no trademark licence or endorsement.
